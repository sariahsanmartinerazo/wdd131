Discover Horizons ✈️

Website Planning Document

Site Name

discoverhorizons.com

Discovering México and the world

Discover Horizons was chosen because it represents the excitement of exploring new destinations, learning about different cultures, and discovering beautiful places around the world.

Site purpose

This website will provide useful information for people who want to travel within México and internationally. It will include travel guides, required travel documents, transportation tips, budgeting advice, safety recommendations, and cultural etiquette. The website will also feature beautiful photos of special places, natural landscapes, historical landmarks, and different cultures around the world to inspire visitors to explore new places.

Scenarios

  • How can I prepare for my international trip?
  • Which destinations offer amazing landscapes and unique cultural experiences?

Color Schema

The color schema was selected to represent adventure, exploration, nature, and cultural discovery.

  • Mountain Green #2D6A4F Header, footer, navigation, main titles
  • Jungle Green #40916C Buttons, accents, hover effects
  • Sunset Orange #F77F00 Highlights and special sections
  • Golden Sun #FFB703 Icons, decorative elements
  • Sky Background#F8F9FA Page background
  • Volcano Text #212529 Paragraphs and general text

Typography

  • Lora - Used for headings and titles.
  • Roboto - Used for paragraphs and general content.
